# Clinical Policy Bulletin: Anti-TNF Biological Therapies for Rheumatoid Arthritis
Policy Number: CPB-0315
Target Therapeutics: Adalimumab (Humira), Etanercept (Enbrel), Infliximab (Remicade)
Applicable Procedure Codes: HCPCS J0135, J1438, J1745, CPT 96372
Applicable Diagnosis Codes: ICD-10 M05.79, M05.89, M06.00, M06.09, M06.9

## 1. Clinical Policy Overview
Anti-Tumor Necrosis Factor (Anti-TNF) biologic therapy is considered medically necessary for adult members aged 18 years or older with active rheumatoid arthritis when criteria in Sections 2.1, 2.2, and 2.3 are verified in clinical documentation.

## 2. Coverage Criteria Requirements

### Section 2.1: Diagnosis and Disease Severity
The member must have:
- Confirmed diagnosis of moderate-to-severe active Rheumatoid Arthritis rendered or co-managed by a board-certified Rheumatologist; AND
- Evidence of active inflammatory disease demonstrated by at least one of the following:
  1. Elevated acute phase reactants: Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR > 28 mm/hr) or C-Reactive Protein (CRP > 1.0 mg/dL); OR
  2. Persistent swelling and tenderness in at least 5 peripheral joints (e.g., wrists, MCPs, PIPs); OR
  3. Clinical Disease Activity Index (CDAI) > 10 or DAS28-CRP score > 3.2.

### Section 2.2: Conventional Synthetic DMARD Failure or Intolerance
Documentation must demonstrate:
- An inadequate response to at least three (3) consecutive months of therapeutic dosing of Methotrexate (minimum target dose >= 15 mg/week oral or subcutaneous); OR
- In cases of documented Methotrexate contraindication or severe intolerance (e.g., liver disease, cytopenias), trial and failure of at least one alternative csDMARD (e.g., Leflunomide 20 mg/day or Sulfasalazine 2000 mg/day) for >= 3 months.

### Section 2.3: Mandatory Infectious Screening & Safety Clearances
Prior authorization requires baseline safety evaluation completed within the prior twelve (12) months:
- Negative Tuberculosis (TB) screening: QuantiFERON-TB Gold blood assay or Tuberculin Skin Test (TST / PPD). If latent TB is identified, documented initiation of appropriate prophylactic antimicrobial therapy is required prior to biologic administration.
- Negative Hepatitis B screening (HBsAg and anti-HBc) and Hepatitis C antibody test.
- Absence of active, unresolved serious systemic bacterial or fungal infection.

### Section 2.4: Contraindications and Exclusions
Authorization is contraindicated and will be DENIED if:
- Patient has severe congestive heart failure (NYHA Class III or IV).
- Patient has concurrent demyelinating disease (such as Multiple Sclerosis).
- Patient is receiving concurrent treatment with another biologic DMARD or targeted synthetic DMARD (e.g., JAK inhibitor).
