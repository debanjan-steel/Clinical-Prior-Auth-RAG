# Clinical Policy Bulletin: Lumbar Spinal Fusion (Arthrodesis)
Policy Number: LCD-L35452
Jurisdiction: CMS / Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC)
Applicable Procedure Codes: CPT 22612, 22630, 22633, 63047
Applicable Diagnosis Codes: ICD-10 M54.50, M54.5, M43.16, M48.061, M51.26

## 1. Description and Clinical Indications
Lumbar spinal fusion is considered medically necessary for skeletally mature patients when ALL of the following core criteria (Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, and 2.4) are satisfied.

## 2. Mandatory Clinical Criteria for Prior Authorization

### Section 2.1: Chronicity and Functional Impairment
The patient must demonstrate:
- Unremitting low back pain with or without lower extremity radiculopathy persisting for at least six (6) consecutive months.
- Documented significant impairment in Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) despite compliant self-care.
- Numeric Pain Rating Scale (NPRS) score >= 6/10 or Oswestry Disability Index (ODI) score >= 40%.

### Section 2.2: Mandatory Conservative Therapy Trial
Prior to surgical authorization, the patient MUST have completed and failed a minimum of six (6) consecutive weeks of structured conservative treatment within the preceding twelve (12) months. Documented conservative therapy MUST include:
1. Physical therapy supervised by a licensed physical therapist (minimum 12 sessions attended); AND
2. Pharmacologic trial of at least two distinct drug classes (e.g., NSAIDs like Naproxen/Meloxicam, muscle relaxants, or Gabapentinoids) unless contraindicated or not tolerated; AND
3. Activity modification and documented home exercise program.
*Exception*: Emergency progressive neurologic deficit (e.g., acute foot drop or cauda equina syndrome with bowel/bladder dysfunction) waives the 6-week conservative trial.

### Section 2.3: Objective Imaging Correlation
Diagnostic imaging (MRI or high-resolution CT completed within the last 12 months) must directly correlate with physical examination findings and establish one of the following:
- Spondylolisthesis (Meyerding Grade II or higher) with documented segmental instability on flexion/extension lateral radiographs (>= 4mm translation or >= 10 degrees angular motion); OR
- Severe lumbar spinal canal stenosis with neural element compression corresponding to clinical symptoms; OR
- Recurrent disc herniation at the same vertebral level with confirmed instability following prior discectomy.

### Section 2.4: Exclusionary Criteria
Prior authorization will be DENIED if any of the following are present:
- Active systemic infection or localized spinal infection (osteomyelitis, discitis).
- Severe psychosocial factors or untreated psychiatric condition contributing to pain behavior.
- Active untreated substance abuse disorder.
- Morbid obesity (BMI >= 45) without documented multidisciplinary surgical clearance.
