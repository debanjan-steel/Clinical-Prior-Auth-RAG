# National Coverage Determination: Positron Emission Tomography (PET) Scans for Oncologic Conditions
Policy Number: NCD-220.2
Applicable Procedure Codes: CPT 78815, 78816
Applicable Diagnosis Codes: ICD-10 C34.90, C34.11, C34.31, C78.00

## 1. Description
CMS covers FDG-PET scans for diagnosis, staging, and restaging of solid tumors when clinical criteria in Section 2 are satisfied.

## 2. Prior Authorization Clinical Criteria

### Section 2.1: Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Staging
FDG-PET is considered medically necessary for:
- Initial staging of biopsy-proven or radiographically suspected Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) where curable surgical resection or definitive chemoradiotherapy is being considered.
- Solitary Pulmonary Nodule (SPN) characterization where contrast-enhanced Chest CT shows a nodule >= 8mm with indeterminate malignancy risk and no distant metastasis identified.

### Section 2.2: Timing and Required Pre-requisites
- High-resolution Chest CT with intravenous contrast must be performed within thirty (30) days prior to the PET scan request.
- No prior whole-body PET scan performed within the preceding 60 days unless restaging following documented completion of a planned chemotherapy cycle or radiation regimen.

### Section 2.3: Exclusions
- Routine surveillance in asymptomatic patients who have completed curative therapy and show no clinical or radiographic evidence of disease recurrence.
- Evaluation of suspected pulmonary nodules smaller than 8mm due to low sensitivity of PET for sub-centimeter lesions.
